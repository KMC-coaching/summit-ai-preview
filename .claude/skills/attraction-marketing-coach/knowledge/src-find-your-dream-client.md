# Training: Find Your Dream Client (Krista Mashore training)

## The one big idea
Everything in your marketing, including messaging, ads, videos, positioning, pricing and offer, depends on knowing exactly who your ideal dream client is. Krista says to take it very seriously, build the blueprint in detail, and treat the avatar as a real person so every decision speaks directly to them.

## Named ideas, frameworks and terms
- **Dream client blueprint**: a detailed map of who you are meant to help so you become their no-brainer solution. Also called the client avatar. You can build it by asking an AI chat tool to map who you help, what they struggle with, what they want, and why you are the obvious choice.
- **Demographics plus lifestyle**: she says both give clarity and confidence.
- **Jack of all trades, master of none**: her reason to choose a specific client and niche (the niche training comes next).
- **Make everyone else irrelevant**: speaking so directly to your client that you come to mind before they need you.

## The steps or process she teaches
Krista lists what to define for your dream client:
1. Age and generation.
2. Gender.
3. Location (city, suburb, zip code, region).
4. Marital status.
5. Kids or no kids, how many and ages.
6. Cultural or ethnic background.
7. Language and religion.
8. Stage of life (new parents, mid-career, empty nesters, retirees).
9. Daily lifestyle (what a typical day looks like).
10. Income level.
11. Education level.
12. Job title or profession.
13. Homeownership (rent or own, apartment, house or condo).
14. Financial mindset (savers, spenders, investors, budgeters).
15. Digital behavior (social media, online shopping, apps, which platforms).
16. Buying confidence (comfortable investing in themselves).
She also says to know their pain points, what they want, their aspirations, what keeps them up at night, what they tried that did not work, what you have that does, and how that is different. Then give your avatar a name.

## Numbers and claims she makes
- Per Krista, a mentor of hers spent about four hours of a two-day private client training just on ideal client and niche.
- Per Krista, this work used to take days of filling in long documents (nine pages for the ideal client, seven for niche, and another for customer and competitor). An AI tool can now do it in a few hours, or minutes, but she wants you to spend a couple of hours to a day.
- Per Krista, she has spent thousands of hours on learning marketing, and built a $70 million business in seven years (her own claims).
- Her own avatar, made seven years ago, has shifted from ages 28 to 58 to about 40 to 60.

## Her stories and examples
- **Two dentists**: a dentist for children's braces and one for a person in their fifties wanting a full smile rebuild have different messaging, income, and even who you speak to (parents versus adults). Lesson: same profession, different avatar.
- **Naming the avatar**: she says a big-stage team she spoke for referred to their ideal client by a first name, "Julie," in every document and meeting, asking what she would do and feel. Lesson: treat the avatar like a real person.
- **Her own copy**: she made a personal award copy, put it in her bathroom and did a vision board. She says it seems corny but she did everything she teaches.
- **Her own avatar sheet**: she showed hers, now with more pages, as an example to keep in front of you.

## What to do next
- Build your dream client blueprint with an AI chat tool, and write everything down.
- Spend a couple of hours to a day, not a few minutes.
- Keep your avatar in front of you, even as a board.
- Ask "what would my avatar do?" with every email, ad and program.
- Write out your dream client profile, then move on to choosing your niche.
