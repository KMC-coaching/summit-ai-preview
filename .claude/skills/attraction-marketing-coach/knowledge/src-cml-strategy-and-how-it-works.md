# Training: How the Community Market Leader Strategy Works (Krista Mashore training)

## The one big idea
The old way was to get people off the platforms and into your list with a lead magnet. Krista says consumer behavior has changed, so the first job now is to get exposure and be seen over and over, building a one-sided, familiar relationship before anyone opts in. Silence from your audience does not mean it is not working.

## Named ideas, frameworks and terms
- **Community market leader**: the professional people recognize and think of in their local area, a bit like a mini local celebrity.
- **Parasocial relationship**: a one-sided relationship where people feel they know and trust you from your content, while you do not know they are watching.
- **Engagement marketing**: attracting people by adding value, serving and helping rather than selling.
- **Lead magnet**: something offered free in exchange for contact information. She says it still works but not as well as it used to, and the process is different now.
- **CPL (cost per lead)**: what it costs to get one contact. She says it varies by industry.
- **CRM and workflows/funnels**: where people eventually land after opting in so you can market to them.
- **Brand awareness strategy**: producing content and paying to put it in front of people, because reach and impressions cost far less than leads.

## The steps or process she teaches
1. Capture attention first, with content that adds value and serves.
2. Keep showing up in people's feeds repeatedly through targeting and retargeting, so you come to mind when they need you.
3. Let the relationship build, expecting silent viewers, no likes, no comments and no opt-ins at first.
4. Eventually get their contact information, opt them into workflows and funnels, and get them into your CRM to market to them.

## Numbers and claims she makes
- Per Krista, referrals go up, including from people who only know you through your marketing.

## Her stories and examples
- **Afraid to answer the phone**: she asks you to think about your own behavior, wary of cloned voices and forms, to explain why people watch without acting.
- **Recognized in public**: people say "aren't you the attorney, dentist or contractor I see everywhere?" That is the result of the strategy.
- **Her own path**: she says this is the same approach she used in real estate, to build her coaching business, and in her network marketing business.
- **Being known**: strangers talk to her wherever she goes, because people feel they know her.
- **How you found her**: you probably watched her for a while, then liked or commented, then showed up to something free, and only later bought.

## What to do next
- There is no homework. Understand it, and know that if you are working it consistently, it is working.
- Stay consistent and keep showing up in your area.
- Do not read a lack of likes, comments or opt-ins as failure.
- Expect the opt-ins and referrals to come later.

## Questions this training answers
- Why does the lead-magnet-first approach not work like it used to?
- What does Krista mean by a parasocial relationship?
- Why are people watching my content but not liking or commenting?
- Why should I pay for exposure before collecting leads?
- Why do big brands spend so much on awareness?
- What does it feel like when the strategy is working?
- When do I get people's contact information?
- Is it still worth doing if I am nervous on camera?
