# Training: The Community Market Leader Sales Cycle Marketing Strategy (Krista Mashore training)

## The one big idea
Krista walks through the whole marketing life cycle of a community market leader in five phases. The early goal is not leads. It is exposure: being seen, building a relationship, and being known as the authority, with video content that actually gets in front of people. Leads, conversion and retention come later, and they cost far less when attention was built first.

## Named ideas, frameworks and terms
- **Cold traffic**: Krista uses "traffic" to mean people. Cold traffic is people who do not know you yet. Phase one is aimed at them.
- **Pattern interrupt hooks**: opening lines and emotional story angles that stop the scroll and make someone want to keep watching.
- **Hook ("eighty percent of your content is in the hook")**: per Krista, most of a video's job is whether the first moment captures attention. She says (loosely, not a verified figure) you have about 1.7 seconds, and the viewer should feel you are talking directly to them (your ideal dream client).
- **Three to five creative variations**: test several hooks and endings on the same message to learn which stops the scroll best.
- **Custom audiences / buckets of people**: groups built from people who watched 3 seconds, 15 seconds, or through plays (50 percent or more), Facebook and Instagram page engagers, Instagram profile visitors, and people who watched 25, 50 and 75 percent of a video.
- **Retarget, retarget, retarget**: keep running ads to the people who already saw you so you stay in front of them. Krista compares it to ads for something you looked at following you around online.
- **Brand awareness campaigns**: ads whose objective is reach, engagement or video views, not conversions. She says many big marketers run these for 30, 60, 90 days before asking for any action.
- **CTR and CPL**: click-through rate and cost per lead, the two numbers she uses to refine lead magnets and ad creative.
- **Lead magnet**: something of value given free in exchange for contact information (checklists, guides, mini webinars, mini trainings).
- **UGC-style videos**: user-generated-content style videos that look and feel like real people made them, not a brand, so they do not feel like constant marketing.

## The steps or process she teaches
1. **Attract (cold traffic).** Build brand awareness with short vertical videos, about 15 to 30 seconds, on Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Choose one platform (she says Facebook most likely). Use reach and engagement or video views as the campaign objective in Ads Manager, not conversions. Build custom audiences from viewers and engagers.
2. **Retarget and keep going.** Keep producing content and running ads through the whole life cycle. This reaches new people, nurtures people who know you, and keeps past clients seeing you.
3. **Lead generation.** Once attention exists, collect leads: native lead form ads on Facebook and Instagram with a clear benefit, landing page conversion ads to free value, and direct DM campaigns through automation. A/B test lead magnets and ad creative each time, and refine by CTR and CPL.
4. **Convert and nurture.** Retarget your CRM with testimonials, value-stacked offers and case studies. Send leads through a nurture sequence with video in the emails. Offer limited-time consultations, reviews or free offers with deadlines. Use DM or SMS urgency only with warm or hot leads. Book qualified appointments with funnels or a scheduling tool.
5. **Retain and multiply.** Keep nurturing with weekly personal video updates and expert tips, ask for reviews with personalized video texts, make client spotlight videos, use UGC-style content, and build a branded community group or newsletter.

## Numbers and claims she makes
- She says capturing attention used to take 12 touches and now takes 47 (a loose spoken figure).
- Per Krista, a viewer's attention window is about 1.7 seconds (a loose spoken claim, not a verified fact).
- Per Krista, "eighty percent of your content is in the hook" (a loose spoken claim, not a verified fact).
- She says the first 60 days are about consistent content, knowing the ideal client, a unique value proposition and a niche.

## Her stories and examples
- **Golf clubs ads**: you look at golf clubs once and the ads follow you everywhere. That is retargeting, and it is what she teaches you to do locally.
- **Lost accounts**: she has had accounts hijacked and has seen platforms disappear overnight. The lesson: own your contacts (your own contact list or CRM), because a platform can vanish.
- **Silent binge watchers**: people will watch you for a long time without liking or commenting, then say they have followed you forever.

## What to do next
- Pick one platform and create short videos consistently, using strong hooks.
- Test three to five hooks and endings on one message.
- Run reach or video-view ads so people see you repeatedly, then retarget.
- Do not judge success by opt-ins early. Be patient.
- In your first 60 days, focus on consistent content, your ideal client, a unique value proposition and a niche.
