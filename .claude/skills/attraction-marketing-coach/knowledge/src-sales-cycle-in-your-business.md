# Training: The Sales Cycle in Your Business (Krista Mashore training)

## The one big idea
Marketing touches nearly every part of the sales cycle, and a missing stage leaves major gaps. Krista teaches the stages in order, tells you to assess how you are doing in each, and says that whenever business has problems you should go back to the sales cycle. She says you do not need more leads, you need more high quality leads that convert.

## Named ideas, frameworks and terms
- **The sales cycle**: Marketing and awareness, lead generation, lead nurture, conversion, results (fulfillment and delivery), then ascension.
- **Marketing and awareness**: The foundation. If you are not marketing well, you cannot give many people your results, however good you are.
- **Refer, retain, resell**: The last stage, done through rituals and routines. Also described as relist and keep using you.
- **House analogy**: Mindset is the ground, marketing and awareness is the foundation, and missing parts mean an incomplete house.
- **Community market leader**: How she teaches you to break through marketing noise.
- **Top of mind awareness**: Staying visible through CRM follow-up and showing up in clients' feeds so they remember to refer you.
- **Ascension**: Getting clients to the next level, refer you, stay, and buy again.

## The steps or process she teaches
1. Marketing and awareness.
2. Lead generation.
3. Lead nurture ("nurture, nurture, nurture").
4. Conversion.
5. Fulfillment and delivery (getting results, which is up to you; her marketing and messaging must match the results promised).
6. Ascension: refer, retain, resell, supported by a CRM and staying top of mind.

Self-check she gives: How am I doing in marketing, lead generation, nurturing, converting, and refer/retain/resell? What am I doing well, where are my gaps, and how can I fill them?

## Numbers and claims she makes
- She says the old rule of seven touches became twelve, and now she says it may be about 47 times before someone even clicks (a loose spoken number, not a verified fact).
- She says we get thousands of marketing messages a day and every third or fourth feed post is an ad.

## Her stories and examples
- Clients who loved you can forget you: someone sees another professional (her chiropractor example) at the store because you were not top of mind. Lesson: build systems to stay in contact.
- Krista bought a house and cannot remember the closet or blinds people who did great work, and could not find the rug cleaner they wanted to use again. None of them marketed to her, so she could not refer them. Lesson: do not be that business.

## What to do next
- Rate yourself honestly at each stage of the sales cycle.
- Fix the biggest gap first and set up your CRM and reminders for staying in touch.
- Come back to the sales cycle when anything in your business is not working.

## Questions this training answers
- What are the stages of the sales cycle?
- Why do I need high quality leads, not just more leads?
- Why is marketing important if I am great at delivering results?
- Why are clients not referring me?
- How many times does someone need to see me before they act?
- What does refer, retain, resell mean?
- Where should I look when my business is struggling?
- Why does nurturing matter after I get a lead?
