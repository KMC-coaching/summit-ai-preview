# Training: Ideal Dream Client Blueprint Walkthrough (Krista Mashore training)

## The one big idea
Krista demonstrates using an AI chat tool in two steps to build your ideal dream client profile in minutes, then saving it as a file you drop into any AI tool so all your content is written with that client in mind. The key lesson is to read and correct what the AI gives you, because your input determines your output.

## Named ideas, frameworks and terms
- **Step one and step two**: two prompts you run in order in an AI chat tool. Step one has the AI analyze your offer, website and audience. Step two builds the ideal customer profile.
- **Ideal dream client blueprint file**: the corrected profile saved as a PDF to drag and drop into any AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Poe and others).
- **Input equals output**: the better and clearer the information you give, the better the AI's results.
- **General practitioner versus brain surgeon**: the more specific you are, the more you are chosen for that specific need.
- **Multiple avatars**: you can make separate PDFs for different niches, a specific age group, house type or neighborhood.

## The steps or process she teaches
1. Open an AI chat tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Poe or similar).
2. Write step one with your target audience, website or videos, and paste it into the AI. It gives back what it understands about you.
3. Write step two (what you specialize in, who struggles with what, their desires, their fears) and paste it in to get your ideal customer profile.
4. Read the whole result and edit anything inaccurate, such as the audience mix, experience level, where they live and what they feel.
5. Paste the corrected version into a document, download it as a PDF, and name it clearly (for example "my ideal client").
6. Drag the PDF into the AI tool and tell it to use it when creating content, for example asking for 10 SEO-optimized video hooks based on what the client searches online.
7. Take the ideas you like and refine the style, such as fun and punchy.
8. Save the file and reuse it every time, because the tools forget.

## Numbers and claims she makes
- Per Krista, this used to take her over 40 hours with a 12-page document, and now takes minutes.
- She says the file can help create content "for a year in five seconds" (loosely stated).
- She says specific content saves time and money, raises conversions, shortens the sales cycle and raises lifetime value.

## Her stories and examples
- **Real estate agent demo**: she used real estate agents as her audience. The AI gave a good draft but got details wrong (such as experience level, where agents live, and tone), and she edited it. Lesson: do not accept it blindly; train it to be accurate.
- **Hook ideas**: with the file attached, the AI produced video ideas (such as getting leads without cold calling) she liked, which she would then refine.

## What to do next
- Run step one then step two in order.
- Edit the result until it matches your real client.
- Save it as a PDF and attach it whenever you create content.
- Create more than one if you serve more than one niche.
- Then use it to build everything else.
