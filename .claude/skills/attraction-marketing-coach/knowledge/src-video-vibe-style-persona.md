# Training: Your Video Vibe and Style Persona (Krista Mashore training)

## The one big idea
Choose one consistent vibe for your business videos so people know what to expect from you. Consistency builds trust, recognition and retention, attracts the right people and repels the wrong ones, and it makes content creation easier. It does not mean you can never be different, only that your tone should feel like you.

## Named ideas, frameworks and terms
**Video vibe / style persona:** the energy your audience can expect from you most of the time. It should be clear, simple to remember and emotionally recognizable.

**Congruency:** people should learn what to expect from you; have a few buckets of content rather than going all over the place.

**Stick-to-it-ness:** consistency keeps people coming back; confusion makes them leave.

**The five personas:**
- **The educator:** teaches, explains and simplifies in a clear, confident, helpful tone. Krista says this is her own style, upbeat and educational. Examples: what to know before hiring a lawyer; top reasons solar saves money.
- **The entertainer:** uses humor, stories and trends to hook people. Good for relatable brands and younger audiences, and can be combined with educating.
- **The motivator:** leads with heart, mindset and inspiration. Empathetic and energizing; she says it suits coaches and service-driven leaders.
- **The behind-the-scenes expert:** shows how you do what you do, transparent and detail-rich. Good for high-trust industries such as legal, home services and medical.
- **The compare and contrast analyst:** helps people choose by breaking options down, such as lease versus buy or agent versus selling alone.

## The steps or process she teaches
Her homework gives the steps:
1. Decide your natural video style.
2. Decide how you want people to feel when they visit your page or channel.
3. List three topics you can start with that match that style.

## Numbers and claims she makes
No numbers. She says that consistency makes people trust you, remember you after one or two pieces, and keep watching, and that this brings referrals because people know what kind of content you make. These are her claims from experience.

## Her stories and examples
- **A dentist who made funny skits with clients.** People went to watch them. The lesson: entertainment can work for a professional with a relatable brand.
- **A plastic surgeon who showed before and after at one week, 30 days and four months.** It educates and shows. The lesson: behind-the-scenes content builds trust in high-trust fields.
- **Krista as an educator.** She breaks steps down in an easy manner, and people follow her for that. The lesson: a clear persona attracts a clear audience and referrals.

## What to do next
- Pick your natural video vibe (or a main one plus a couple of supporting buckets).
- Write down how you want viewers to feel.
- List three topics that fit your style and start on them.
- Remember you can still do the occasional funny or serious video; just keep your tone feeling like you. You can also change over time.
- Do not try to be everything to everyone.
