# Training: Lead Follow-Up Strategies (Krista Mashore training)

## The one big idea
All the content, funnels and systems you build only pay off if you actually follow up with the leads quickly and persistently. Krista shares lead response research and says speed and repeated attempts matter most, and that she would add text and video text messages on top. Her shorthand is to stop, drop and call, text or video text when a lead comes in.

## Named ideas, frameworks and terms
**Stop, drop and call / text / video text:** when a lead comes in, stop what you are doing and reach out right away by phone, text or video text message.

**Persistence:** making multiple attempts rather than one or two.

**Video text message:** a short personal video sent by text. Per Krista, they dramatically increase show up rate, conversion and pick-up rate.

**Be known before they are ready:** stay visible with consistent content so you are the one they think of when they decide to act.

## The steps or process she teaches
Her practical points:
1. Contact a new lead as fast as possible, ideally within five minutes.
2. Make at least six call attempts.
3. If they do not answer, hang up and call back right away.
4. Send a text first saying who you are and that you are calling, so they are likelier to pick up.
5. Add video text messages.
6. Keep consistent online so you stay known while they research.
The research she cites on timing says Wednesdays and Thursdays are best (Thursday the very best), with 4 to 5 p.m. best and 8 to 9 a.m. second best for reaching and qualifying leads.

## Numbers and claims she makes
These come from studies she summarized (she says from 2023, from multiple studies including research she describes as a large three-year study); treat them as her cited figures.
- Average response time is 47 hours; she says only a small share of leads get a response.
- A one-minute response can lead to a 391% increase in conversions.
- 82% of consumers expect a response within 10 minutes (she says it is probably shorter now).
- Fewer than 25% of businesses call web leads; over 30% of leads are never contacted.
- She cites a study of 15,000 leads and 100,000 call attempts: odds of contacting drop 400% from five minutes to 10 minutes; six call attempts give a 90% increase; a few more calls give up to a 70% increase in contact rates.
- Thursday versus Tuesday showed a 49% difference; 4 to 5 p.m. versus 1 to 2 p.m. showed a 164% difference.
- Per Krista, she believes the study did not test text or video text messages, so those are her own addition.
- Homebuyers typically take about three months to buy but start engaging with agents earlier.

## Her stories and examples
- **Homebuyers.** They take around three months, so be known early. The lesson: stay in touch before they are ready.
- **Researching solar.** She and her partner are looking at solar companies, comparing several, with no decision yet; she says if she had been watching a solar professional's content, that person would probably get the bid. The lesson: content keeps you top of mind during the research phase.
- **Calling and hanging up.** Her team calls, hangs up if there is no answer, and calls right back. The lesson: it prompts them to answer.

## What to do next
- Write the statistics up and post them on your wall.
- Have the five things in place: systems, processes, follow-up, consistency and focus, plus commitment.
- Call or text new leads right away and plan at least six attempts.
- Add text and video text messages.
- Keep using your online presence to stay known.
