# Training: Defining Your Market, Niche and Ideal Dream Client (Krista Mashore training)

## The one big idea
Your market, niche and ideal dream client are the foundation of every business, like the foundation of a high rise. Krista says to put about 80% of your effort into knowing who your audience is and what problem you solve in a niche way. Then you use ChatGPT to build a dream client profile in minutes instead of weeks, and save it so you can use it every time you create content.

## Named ideas, frameworks and terms
- **Foundation**: Your market and niche, then your offer, positioning, content and marketing. If the foundation is off, ads, messaging and lead quality all suffer.
- **Five pillars of a great market**: You love helping these people and love the market; they are in deep pain; they have deep pockets with that deep pain; there is growth; and they are easily accessible.
- **Demand and market size**: There is a clear need and a big enough pool of potential clients locally. Look for under-served local services that already have strong national demand.
- **Competition and differentiation**: A good market has competition, and you also need to be able to stand out. Market versus niche: real estate is the market, first-time home buyers or a certain neighborhood is the niche.
- **Pricing and "bigger, better, stronger, faster"**: Clients must be able to afford what you offer. Check what competitors charge and whether there is room for a premium offer.
- **Local trends of consumer behavior**: New local trends and unmet needs (she mentions eco-friendly homes and solar as examples). Events and conferences in your industry can hint at what is coming.
- **Accessibility and reach**: Can you physically and digitally reach this group? Where do they spend time online?
- **CLV (customer lifetime value)**: A great market is about repeat and referral business, not one sale. For agents, she says doing a good job and staying top of mind should bring repeat transactions.
- **Target audience readiness and alignment**: Is the market already wanting what you offer, and do their values match yours?
- **Dream client blueprint**: A prompt you paste into an AI chat tool such as ChatGPT so it maps who you help, what they struggle with, what they want, and why you are the obvious choice.

## The steps or process she teaches
1. Review the five pillars and the market factors above for your market.
2. Open ChatGPT (she prefers the strongest reasoning model) or use Poe, which gives you several AI tools in one place. A paid ChatGPT plan affects which you use.
3. Write a dream client blueprint prompt asking it to map who you help, what they struggle with, what they want, and why you are the obvious choice. Fill in your target audience, your product details, and anything you already have (a website, PDF, video, script or book). Tell it before you upload more than one item.
4. Add a real description of who you work with and what they want.
5. Tell ChatGPT to commit it to memory.
6. Read the output (name, age, family, fears, private thoughts, what they have tried, what they really want). Correct anything that is off by telling it what to change.
7. Download it as a PDF and save it on your desktop so you can drag and drop it into AI chats whenever you create content.

## Numbers and claims she makes
- Per Krista, her own avatar document was 11 pages of questions and used to take more than a week; the prompt does it in seconds.
- She says most local professionals do not spend enough time here and miss the mark.
- She says the average expected repeat business for agents is "seven" more transactions (she attributes this loosely to an industry rule of thumb).

## Her stories and examples
- Her own avatar prompt targets local professionals who are not brick and mortar (realtors, lenders, insurance agents, lawyers, dentists, chiropractors, solar reps). ChatGPT first gave an insurance agent persona, so she told it to stop focusing on one profession and write for all local professionals. Lesson: review and steer the output.
- Her niche is broad now because she spent seven years focused only on real estate agents, so she has authority and a proven concept. Lesson: do not start as broad as she is now; start narrower.

## What to do next
- Write your dream client prompt and run it.
- Edit the profile until it sounds right, save the PDF, and reuse it with every content prompt.
- Do not worry about perfection in the first pass.
