# Training: How Prospects Move Through Your System (Krista Mashore training)

## The one big idea
The funnel is the path that takes a lead from first hearing about you to booking a consultation. When you have a tool that tracks each lead's progress through the funnel, you always know where someone is and can follow up at the right time.

## Named ideas, frameworks and terms
**The funnel:** the process that takes leads from awareness to a booked consultation. The top of the funnel is where you capture interest.

**Tracking leads:** a tool that tracks where each lead is in your funnel automatically, so you are not guessing.

**Awareness:** the lead first comes into contact with your business through social media, your website, a referral or your videos.

**Interest:** the lead engages with your content, for example by downloading a lead magnet or signing up for a newsletter.

**Consideration:** the lead is weighing your services and educating themselves, perhaps checking reviews or watching videos. This is where follow-up comes in.

**Booking:** the lead chooses you and books a consultation at the bottom of the funnel.

## The steps or process she teaches
