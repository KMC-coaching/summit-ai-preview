# Training: What Content to Create to Build Trust and Be the Local Authority (Krista Mashore training)

## The one big idea
The more people see you, the more they like and trust you, so your content should be built to make you known as the go-to local authority. Krista says to build everything around two things: character (who you are) and competency (what you can do for your audience). Storytelling, teaching, proof, community and engagement are the content types that do this.

## Named ideas, frameworks and terms
**Character and competency:** the two things to think about when deciding what content to create. Character is sharing who you are so people connect with you. Competency is showing what you do for your audience and that you are confident at it.

**Make your mess your message:** you can share a struggle publicly, but only after you have fully recovered from it, and for a good while after. Until then, it can hurt how much people trust you as a local expert.

**Facts tell, stories sell:** storytelling is the most effective way to share personal experiences and lifestyle that resonate with your audience. Use it as often as you can.

**Value posts:** content that solves problems before any sale. Krista's pattern is what it is, why they need it, how to teach it, and how they can apply it.

**Result shares:** proof that your product works through real outcomes and measurable results, such as testimonials and case studies with data inside your teaching.

**Community-driven content:** showing you support the community (churches, schools, organizations) so you are seen as a community person, not just your profession.

**Engagement content:** questions, opinions and invitations.

## The steps or process she teaches
She lists content types rather than a strict sequence:
1. Share who you are (character), including your values and your story.
2. Show your competency by demonstrating what you do.
3. Tell stories and share personal experiences.
4. Post value content that solves problems ahead of the sale.
5. Share results with real outcomes and measurable data.
6. Show up for the community.
7. Invite engagement with questions, opinions and invitations.
Then take your ideal client avatar and your content map or value map (a map of where your client is now and where they want to go) into an AI chat tool and ask it to build content that includes as many of these elements as possible.

## Numbers and claims she makes
- Per Krista, "research shows" that the more somebody sees you, the more they start to like you, even if they did not at first (she states this without a specific source).

## Her stories and examples
- **Her own story as proof of the idea.** She asks viewers to notice how they feel about her after watching her trainings, and to ask whether they would feel they knew her if they saw her on the street. The lesson: repeated exposure builds familiarity and trust, and that is what your audience will go through with your videos.
- **Waiting to share a struggle.** Her lesson: people need to trust you and may trust you less if they see you in the middle of a struggle, so speak from conviction after the struggle is behind you (she adds no judgment about the struggle itself).
- **A time-saving result.** She gives as an example a student who saves 16 hours a week by using one of her programs. The lesson: attach a measurable result to what you teach.

## What to do next
- Let an AI tool do much of this work for you (she says it can).
- Pull together your ideal client avatar and your content map or value map, then ask the AI to create content that includes these elements.
- Decide which of your stories and values to share, and hold back anything you are still working through.
- Look for testimonials, case studies and measurable results you can weave into teaching.
- Find ways to show up for community organizations.
