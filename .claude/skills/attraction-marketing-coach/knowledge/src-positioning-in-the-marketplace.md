# Training: Positioning in the Marketplace (Krista Mashore training)

## The one big idea
Positioning is how your market sees you before you ever pitch. Krista's goal is to "win before you arrive": people should know and trust you before they need you, so that whenever they think of your profession in your community, you come to mind. Strong positioning shortens the sales cycle and supports higher prices.

## Named ideas, frameworks and terms
- **Win before you arrive**: Build trust and familiarity ahead of the need, so you are not the stranger they meet only when they are ready to buy.
- **Community market leader / local go-to expert**: The position she wants you to hold in your area, beyond just being a dentist, chiropractor, solar rep, agent or lender.
- **Positioning**: How your market labels you and where your services sit in their mind, before an offer is made. She compares it to high school labels (jocks, skaters, geeks): people will label you either way.
- **Strategic control of the label**: Your market will form an opinion regardless, so you engineer it on purpose.
- **Premium positioning**: Higher perceived value that allows higher prices, like a specialist versus a general practitioner, or the brain surgeon versus the general doctor.
- **Customer value map**: A map that takes your client from where they are to where they want to go, used to create the right content and message for each platform.
- **7-11-4 rule**: A rule she mentions that people need about seven hours of exposure, eleven touches, on four platforms. She says today it is more.

## The steps or process she teaches
She gives a sequence of what comes next rather than strict steps:
1. Understand why positioning matters.
2. Identify your ideal dream client using a prompt.
3. Build a value map from that.
4. Use it to make content for each platform, with the right message for the right buyer at the right time.

## Numbers and claims she makes
- Per Krista, strong positioning can bring "three times" more conversions, or more, double your prices, shorten the sales cycle, lower cost per lead and raise customer lifetime value (her loose spoken claims).
- She cites outside research that positioning can cut the sales cycle by 50% or more, supports premium pricing, lowers acquisition cost, and that 73% of consumers are more likely to buy from brands they see as trusted authorities.
- She says the exposure rule came from a book in 2007 and that with hundreds more platforms now, getting attention is easier yet harder to win.

## Her stories and examples
- A dentist who positions as a specialist can charge more than a general practitioner. Lesson: be the brain surgeon, not the generalist.
- High school labels: people sort you whether you like it or not.

## What to do next
- Decide how you want to be labeled in your community.
- Identify your ideal dream client with the prompt, then create the customer value map.
- Give people helpful content before they need you, solving their big problems before asking for money.

## Questions this training answers

- Why does positioning matter for local professionals?
- What does "win before you arrive" mean?
- How can positioning shorten my sales cycle?
- How can I charge more for my services?
