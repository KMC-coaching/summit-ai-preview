# Training: Validate Your Niche Before You Build (Krista Mashore training)

## The one big idea
Before you create videos, content, an offer or a brand, test that your niche is real and that people want it. Krista teaches a research routine using ChatGPT, Google, YouTube, reviews, Reddit, Pinterest, Google Trends and the Facebook ads library, locally and in other cities, so you can craft an offer no one nearby is making.

## Named ideas, frameworks and terms
- **Niche**: Something you specialize in so that when someone thinks of that need, you come to mind, because your offer is unique and speaks right to them.
- **Category queen (or king)**: Being your own category so you are not compared to anyone.
- **Validate before you build**: Test and prove it before investing in content, offers or branding.
- **Niche within a niche / subcategory**: A narrower lane so people think "no one else is talking about this."
- **Blue ocean**: Your own niche with no competition, in contrast to a crowded market.
- **Emulate and model**: Study what works for others and make it your own. She dislikes the word "hacking" because people hear stealing. Copying word for word is not okay.
- **Emulate and model folder / niche workbook**: A place for screenshots, links, hooks and ChatGPT answers you collect.
- **Competitive market analysis**: Local and outside research on your niche that you work through carefully so you do not rush this step.

## The steps or process she teaches
Test your niche with ChatGPT:
1. Ask for five niche variations for your business, audience and problem.
2. Ask what your dream client would search on Google or YouTube to solve the problem (gives you real language for hooks, captions, offers).
3. Ask what services or products could be created for this audience and problem.
4. Ask for three unique ways to stand out and create a subcategory.
5. Ask for ten video topics that speak to their pain points (this shows if the niche has repeatable content).
6. Screenshot answers into your workbook and ask: does this sound like me and my client?

Then research outside ChatGPT:
7. Google it like a buyer: "best [niche] in [city]", "how to [problem]", "[niche] for [specific person]". Use autocomplete, "people also ask" and related searches as video topic ideas.
8. Check YouTube: titles, views, comments, whether creators post regularly. Make sure no one is doing it locally.
9. Find a successful professional in another city (she suggests at least two hours away) with a local business, and study their brand, voice, offer, hooks and content. Look for gaps you can fill. Also borrow ideas from other industries.
10. Ask your audience: polls and open questions (what would you like help with, what is hardest, what have you tried that did not work). Use their words.
11. Read reviews of top-selling books in your niche on Amazon (what people praise and complain about), browse niche Reddit threads for frustrations and wish lists, and Pinterest for visual pain points and trends.
12. Use Google Trends for upward movement, seasons, geography and related topics.
13. Use the Facebook ads library to see visuals, hooks, offers, calls to action, audience and how long ads have been running.

## Numbers and claims she makes
- She says to look at people at least two hours away so you do not compete.
- She says the longer an ad has been running, the more likely it is working and profitable.
- Per Krista, her students run home buyer and seller seminars; she showed ads in the library that had run only a couple of days.

## Her stories and examples
- When she entered real estate she studied what locals and out-of-area agents did, then borrowed from other industries. Nobody used funnels in real estate, so she built many.
- She built home seller and buyer courses to raise her authority, not to sell the courses, adapting an idea from course creators.
- She screenshots ads and hooks she likes and keeps links and folders labeled emulate and model. Lesson: be a producer and a student of what works, not a scroller.
- Her health coach example: women over 40 with energy, hormones and weight gain, and Google search suggestions for weight loss showed her real search language.

## What to do next
- Google your niche, run the ChatGPT prompts, check YouTube and Instagram.
- Find one professional outside your market to learn from.
- Read three Amazon reviews in your niche and post a poll.
- Complete your competitive market analysis without rushing, then define your niche.
