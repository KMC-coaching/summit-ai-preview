# Training: Becoming a Community Market Leader (Krista Mashore training)

## The one big idea
A community market leader is the person people think of whenever they think about your profession in your community, because you educate and serve through your marketing. Krista teaches that being unique is not enough if nobody knows about you, so you market through education to build a one-sided relationship that makes people trust you before they need you.

## Named ideas, frameworks and terms
- **Community market leader**: Krista's definition is domination and market share in a specific geographic area, reached through excellence, innovation, marketing, education and engagement.
- **The manifesto**: a statement Krista has her students read every day so they start to identify as a community market leader. It has you insert your profession, and covers being a visionary, changing how your profession is done, serving not just selling, a lifelong learner, a positive mindset and acting as if the goal is already achieved.
- **The pledge**: a promise to clients and yourself to give 100 percent with the most integrity.
- **The customer journey**: awareness, then engagement, then conversion, then retention, then advocates.
- **Educate**: showing you are the expert so people trust and respect you as the go-to authority.
- **Parasocial relationship**: a one-sided emotional bond where someone feels they know you through media exposure while there is no real-world interaction. She says it forms through consistent content that is actually seen.
- **Engagement marketing**: marketing by engaging your community with high-value content.
- **Innovation**: being different, communicating an idea differently, setting the trend instead of following it.
- **Serve, not sell**: her line is that the more you serve, the more you sell.

## The steps or process she teaches
She does not give a numbered process, but the order of the customer journey is: awareness, engagement (attention, likes, comments, shares), conversion, retention, then advocacy.

## Numbers and claims she makes
- Per Krista, she gives away everything she teaches.
- She says research shows the more people see you, the more they like you (her statement, no source given).
- She says the more you give away, the more people realize they need help.
- She says getting attention has never been harder.

## Her stories and examples
- **Annoying at first**: some people told her they found her fast-talking energy annoying until they listened and saw she knew her stuff. Lesson: familiarity through consistency builds liking.
- **Big brands that educate**: she cites Fortune 500 companies like Nike, Whole Foods, Apple and Google, and a motel brand that teaches how to check for bed bugs, as brands that build trust by educating. When she started in real estate she studied them and copied the approach.
- **Blockbuster and Netflix**: Blockbuster would not change and a more innovative company overtook it. Lesson: innovation beats the old way.
- **Giving away too much**: some fear that sharing knowledge means people will not need them. She says you can look anything up with AI, but people still need people to get things done.

## What to do next
- Write your own version of the manifesto for your profession (visionary, changing how your profession is done, serving not just selling, lifelong learner, positive mindset, acting as if the goal is already achieved) and read it every day.
- Create content that educates and adds value, and make sure it is seen.
- Be consistent so people know you before they need you.
- Give value without expecting anything back, and do not fear giving away too much.
- Be different and innovative, but pair it with marketing, since being unique alone will not help if no one knows you.
