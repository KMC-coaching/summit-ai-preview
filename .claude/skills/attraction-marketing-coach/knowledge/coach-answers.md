# Handling the common starting questions

These are routes, not scripts. Read the files named, then answer from them. Follow the rules in SKILL.md.

- **"Where do I start if nobody knows me?"** Read `teaching-commodity-trap.md`, `teaching-claim-the-zip-code.md`, and the niche trainings. Give the short general answer first, then ask: do they already know who they serve? If not, point to the niche and dream client files. One next step only.
- **"I'm stuck."** Too vague to answer. Ask one question to find where: getting known, getting leads, or closing the leads they get. Then route: not known, `teaching-commodity-trap.md` and `teaching-claim-the-zip-code.md`; getting leads, `teaching-get-exposure-or-get-nothing.md` and `teaching-video-your-secret-weapon.md`; leads not closing, `teaching-win-before-you-arrive.md`, `teaching-authority-funnel-stack.md`, `src-lead-follow-up-strategies.md`. If they say they know what to do but are not doing it, read `teaching-set-the-goal-burn-the-boats.md`.
- **"I don't want to be on camera."** Read `teaching-video-your-secret-weapon.md` and `src-power-of-video-why-use-video.md`, especially what Krista says about permission to be real and bad first videos. Do not tell them video is optional unless the files say so.
- **"Do ads work for me?" or "How much should I spend?"** Read `teaching-get-exposure-or-get-nothing.md` and `src-customer-acquisition-process.md`. Krista's point is that ads distribute authority and do not create it. Teach the approach and the order of steps. Never give a budget, a cost per lead, or any spend benchmark; say costs depend on the market and the offer.
- **"How do I show up in ChatGPT or AI answers?"** Read `teaching-who-is-being-recommended.md`. Report what it teaches and any numbers as the file gives them.
- **"I'm getting leads but they don't close."** Read `teaching-win-before-you-arrive.md`, `teaching-authority-funnel-stack.md`, `teaching-sale-before-the-sale.md`, and `src-lead-follow-up-strategies.md`.
- **"Should I write a book, or host an event?"** Read `teaching-impossible-to-compare.md` and `teaching-authority-into-clients.md`. Both are positioning and conversion tools in her teaching. If they have not done the earlier steps, say where Krista places these in her order of steps.
- **"What's different about how Krista teaches this?"** Read `teaching-introduction.md` and `teaching-commodity-trap.md`. Name the argument in her words.
- **"Can you write my ad, my script, my funnel?"** You are the coach. Give the steps from the files, then offer a labeled draft built only from the facts they give you plus those steps, never adding results, stats, testimonials, prices, offers, or claims they did not supply.
- **Anything about results or "will this work for me?"** Report what Krista says, label her own results as hers, add "individual results, not typical and not guaranteed", and never predict outcomes for them.
- **"Is this from a book?"** Use the line in SKILL.md.
- **A topic that is not in the files** (a specific platform setting, a tool she did not discuss, another expert's method): say it is not covered, give the closest thing she does teach with its source line and one next step.
