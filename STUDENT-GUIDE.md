# Your Summit AI Preview — how to start

You'll need about 15 minutes, a computer (Mac or Windows), and Google Chrome.
You will not type any commands. Claude does the work.

## Before you start

1. **Finish the Summit workbook** and click its **download** button at the end.
   Don't rename the file and don't move it. Claude finds it on its own.
2. **Have the Claude desktop app, signed in.** If you went through Module 1, you
   already do. If not: download it from claude.ai/download, open it, and sign in.
   The part we use (the **Code** tab) needs a paid Claude plan.

## Start it

1. Open the Claude app and click the **Code** tab.
2. Start a **new session**. If it asks you to pick a folder, pick **Documents**.
3. Copy everything in the box below, paste it in, and press Enter.

```text
Set up my Summit AI Preview.

1. Download it from https://github.com/KMC-coaching/summit-ai-preview. It's public, so
   no account or sign-in is needed. Get it as a zip with curl, not git (git may not be
   installed yet): https://codeload.github.com/KMC-coaching/summit-ai-preview/zip/refs/heads/main
   Unpack it into a folder called Summit-AI-Preview in my home folder. If that folder
   already exists, keep everything in its app/data folder and update the rest.
2. Open START-HERE.md in that folder and follow it exactly, from Step 0.

I'm not technical. Talk to me in plain English and never ask me to type commands.
```

## While it runs

- **Boxes asking to allow something:** pick the option that allows it and **doesn't ask
  again**.
- **A window from Apple asking to install tools:** click **Install**. It's slow (up to
  ten minutes) and looks frozen. That's normal.
- **"Claude would like to access your Downloads folder":** click **Allow**. That's how
  it finds your workbook file.
- **If a link asks where to open:** always choose **Open in Google Chrome**.
- **Stuck?** Take a screenshot, paste it into Claude, and ask "what do I do next?"

## When it's done

Your preview opens in Google Chrome. Click around: your website report card, your sample
website, your first lead magnet, and your next few days, all built from your answers.

Lost the tab? Go back to Claude and say **open my preview**.
