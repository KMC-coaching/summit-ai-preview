# 03. Unique Differentiator

## The system: The No-Surprises Seller Method

Jordan picked this name from three options:
- The No-Surprises Seller Method (picked)
- The Ten-Day Listing Launch Plan
- The Fix-or-Skip Listing System

**What it is:** a written plan a seller can hold in their hands before they sign anything. It covers what to fix, what to skip, what to ask for, and what happens on each of the first ten days.

**Why it is different:** most agents talk about the plan. Jordan writes it down first.

## How Jordan works, in Jordan's words

"Walkthrough first, then a written fix-or-skip list, then a pricing talk with the numbers on the table, then I write out the first ten days of the listing before we sign anything, then a written update every week."

"Most agents list it and wait. I plan the first ten days before we sign, and I don't push people into repairs that don't pay back."

What clients get: they stop worrying about it, and they know what is happening the whole time.

## What the system does

1. **Attract.** Jordan shows up where long-time owners already are, with plain market numbers and a calm voice.
2. **Nurture.** Before anyone is ready to sell, they get something useful and written. No pressure.
3. **Convert.** The walkthrough and the fix-or-skip list show the seller the plan before they commit.
4. **Follow up.** A written update every week while the home is listed, and a reason to stay in touch after.

## The seller steps

1. **The walkthrough.** Jordan walks the house with the owner before anything is decided.
2. **The fix-or-skip list.** A written list. Fix what pays back. Skip what doesn't.

4. **The ten-day launch plan.** The first ten days of the listing, written out, before the seller signs.
5. **The weekly written update.** Every week, in writing, what happened and what is next.
