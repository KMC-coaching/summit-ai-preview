About Jordan Avery

Calm, prepared, and on your side of the table

Jordan Avery helps long-time owners in East Sacramento, Land Park and Elk Grove sell with a plan.

I work mostly with people who have been in their homes a long time. Selling a house like that is not a transaction. It is a lot of years, and it deserves a plan.

So before we sign anything, I write out the first ten days of the listing. You know what happens, when it happens, and why.

The First Ten Days

You see the first ten days of your listing in writing before you sign anything.

01

The walkthrough

We walk the house together before anything else happens, room by room, with a notepad and no sales pitch.

02

Fix or skip

A written list of what is worth doing before you list and what is not. No repairs that will not pay you back.
