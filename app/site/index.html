The difference

A written plan before the sign goes up

Listing a home you have lived in for years is a lot to hand over to someone. You should know what happens on day one, day three and day ten before you agree to any of it.

Questions

What people ask first

What should I fix before I sell?

Often less than you think. After the walkthrough you get a written fix-or-skip list, so you only spend money on what will come back to you.

How do you decide the listing price?

We put recent nearby sales next to your home on one page and decide together. You will understand the number before it goes on the listing.

How much disruption will showings be?

The showing schedule is part of the ten-day plan, agreed before launch, so you know when people are coming through.

How will I know what is happening once it is listed?

You get a short written update every week: who came through, what they said, and the next step.

Do you work with buyers too?

Yes, especially first-time buyers in Elk Grove and South Sacramento who want each step explained.
