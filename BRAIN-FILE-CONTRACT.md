# The workbook file — what the Summit workbook must download

This is the hand-off between Krista's Summit workbook and this preview. The workbook
asks the questions; this preview never asks them again. It finds the file on its own.

## 1. The file name (the only hard rule)

**`summit-ai-brain.md`**, saved by the browser's normal download to **Downloads**.

- Do not ask the person to rename it or move it.
- Browsers add ` (1)`, ` (2)` on repeat downloads, and Safari sometimes adds `.txt`.
  That is fine: the finder takes the **newest** file whose name contains `ai-brain`,
  `summit…brain` or `summit…workbook`, ending in `.md`, `.txt`, `.json`, `.pdf` or
  `.docx`, from Downloads, Desktop or Documents, from the last 120 days.
- Keep `summit-ai-brain` at the start of the name and every variant is found.

## 2. The format

Plain Markdown. One `##` heading per section below, then each question and its answer.
Exact wording does not matter: Claude reads it, it is not parsed by a machine. What
matters is that the answers are there. Blank answers are fine; the preview leaves a
visible placeholder rather than inventing anything.

```markdown
# Summit AI Brain — Maria Lopez
Saved: 2026-10-20

## About you
- Your name: ...
```

## 3. What it needs to contain

Starred items drive something on the dashboard directly. Without them that panel is
thinner, not broken.

### About you and your business
- ★ Your name
- ★ Business name
- ★ What you do, in plain words (profession)
- ★ Where you work (city / area, or "online")
- Phone and email you want on your website (optional)
- Social media handles (optional)
- **Real estate only:** license number, brokerage name, and the neighborhoods or towns
  you work most. *(The license number and brokerage go in the footer of every page, as
  the law requires. If they are blank, the preview shows a blank to fill in. It never
  makes one up.)*
- **Any real numbers you are proud of** (homes sold, years in business, clients served).
  Optional. *(Shown as animated stats only if given. Never invented.)*

### Your website
- ★ Your current website address, or "I don't have one"
- ★ If you don't have one: a competitor's site, or a site you admire in your field
  *(this is what the report card grades; the person does not need to know why we ask)*

### Your brand
- ★ Three to five words for how you want to come across
- Your brand colors, if you have them (hex codes or plain names)
- A sentence or two you'd actually say to a client (a voice sample)
- Words or phrases you use a lot / words you never want to use
- Your tagline, if you have one

### Your ideal client (client avatar)
- ★ Who is your ideal client? Age, situation, what is going on in their life
- ★ Their three biggest frustrations or fears
- ★ What they want most
- Where they spend time (online and off)

### What makes you different (unique differentiator)
- ★ Why should someone pick you over everyone else who does what you do?
- How you work, step by step, if you have a process (even an unnamed one)
- What you never do that others in your field do

### Your services
- ★ What you offer, each with who it is for and what they walk away with

### Your business right now (feeds the AI opportunity audit)
- ★ How do new clients find you today?
- ★ Do you have anything free that collects contact details (guide, checklist, quiz)? How many?
- ★ How do you follow up with a lead who doesn't buy straight away?
- ★ After a call or meeting, do you send a recap or follow-up? Always, sometimes, rarely?
- ★ What eats most of your week that you wish it didn't?
- How often do you post on social media?
- Which email and calendar do you use? (Gmail, Outlook, other)
- Your biggest goal for the next 90 days

## 4. If the file is missing

The preview offers to ask these same questions itself and saves the answers as
`~/Downloads/summit-ai-brain.md`, so the rest of the run is identical. That is the
fallback, not the plan: the workbook is meant to collect them.
