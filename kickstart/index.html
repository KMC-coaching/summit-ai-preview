VIP and Fast Action

VIP Kickstart Setup

Your workbook answers become your AI Preview: a demo of your website, your lead magnets, a report card and your AI game plan. About 15 minutes. You copy and paste once, and Claude does the rest.

Before you start: your workbook file

At the bottom of your workbook page, click Save and Download. A file named AIDR-Workbook_ and your name lands in your Downloads folder.

Don't rename it, don't move it, and don't open it to change anything. Claude finds it there on its own.

No file yet? Stop here. Go back and finish your workbook.

  1. You need a paid Claude plan

    Claude Pro or higher. The free plan can't do this part.

    See Claude plans

  2. Get the Claude app on your computer

    Download it, install it, and sign in. Mac or Windows.

    Download Claude

  3. Start a new session

    Open the Claude app. Click Code at the top, then New session. If it asks you to pick a folder, pick Desktop.

  4. Copy and paste this, then press Enter

    You don't even need to read this. Just copy and paste it.

    Build my AI Preview from my workbook file in my Downloads folder.
Use curl to download https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KMC-coaching/summit-ai-preview/main/START-HERE.md
then read it and follow it exactly. I'm not technical, so never ask me to type commands.

  5. While it works (about 10 minutes)

    • A box asks to allow something: pick the option that allows it and doesn't ask again.
    • Your Mac asks if Claude can look in Downloads: click Allow. That's how it finds your workbook.
    • A window from Apple asks to install tools: click Install. It's slow and looks frozen. That's normal.
    • A link asks where to open: choose Open in Google Chrome.

  6. When it's done

    Your preview opens in Google Chrome. Lost the tab? In Claude, say open my preview.

    Finished more of your workbook later? Download it again and paste the same message. Your preview rebuilds with everything.

Your preview lives only on your computer. Nothing is published or sent anywhere.
